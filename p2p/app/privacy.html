P!nterest2Plate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

P!nterest2Plate (hosted at https://pinterest-to-plate.vercel.app) helps users turn their saved recipe pins into organized meal plans and grocery shopping lists for UK supermarkets.

2. Pinterest Data & Scopes

When connecting your Pinterest account via OAuth 2.0, our application only requests read access (boards:read, pins:read) to access the names of your boards and the recipe pins you select. We do not modify, delete, or create pins or boards on your account without explicit confirmation.

3. Data Storage

User pantry exclusions and recipe selections are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. We do not sell or monetize personal browsing history.

4. Supermarket Links

Outbound links to supermarkets (Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda, Aldi, Lidl) are standard search queries intended to reduce decision fatigue.
