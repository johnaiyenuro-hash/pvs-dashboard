5-Stage Vision Engine Pipeline
01
Visual Ingestion
OCR scans infographic text, handwriting & TikTok/Reels captions.
02
Semantic Normalization
Converts "3 dollops" or "a handful" into standard metric units (grams, packs, whole).
03
Multi-Recipe Deduplication
Merges overlapping items across 3 meals (e.g. 8 cloves garlic → 1 fresh head).
04
Pantry Staple Filter
Detects kitchen staples (salt, oil, flour) and remembers user cupboard inventory.
05
Supermarket Cart Injection
Maps each item to real SKUs in Tesco, Sainsbury's, and Asda with affiliate tracking.
Average Vision Processing Time: 1.2 seconds / recipe

2. Strategic Profitability Engine (The 4 Phases)

Path to £10,000+ MRR
PHASE 1 // WEEKS 1–4 BOOTSTRAP

Zero-Capital Launch & Organic Viral Wedge

Validate demand without spending a penny on advertising. Tap into existing TikTok & Pinterest food communities where users complain about having hundreds of bookmarks they never cook.

  • ✔ Deploy Vercel web app with instant Pinterest board ingest.
  • ✔ The Viral TikTok Hook: Post 15s screen recordings showing: "POV: You have 600 saved recipes on Pinterest... watch this tool put them in my Tesco cart in 10 seconds."
  • ✔ Milestone: 500 initial active weekly cookers.
PHASE 2 // MONTHS 2–4 MONETIZATION 1

Supermarket Affiliate & Bounty Network

Supermarkets operate massive affiliate bounty programs through networks like Awin. You earn every time a user checks out an online delivery basket.

  • ✔ Sign up to Awin (hosts Sainsbury's, Asda, and Morrisons grocery affiliate programs in the UK).
  • ✔ Unit Economics: £3.50–£8.00 bounty per new online grocery customer; 2%–3% commission on existing shopper basket totals.
  • ✔ Milestone: £2,500/month recurring affiliate revenue from 400 weekly supermarket orders.
PHASE 3 // MONTHS 4–6 MONETIZATION 2

"Plate Pro" Consumer Subscription (£3.99/mo)

Free tier allows 3 recipes/week. Power cooks who want unlimited pin syncing, macro/calorie counts, and automated cart injection subscribe to Pro.

  • ✔ Stripe checkout integration for £3.99/month or £34.99/year.
  • ✔ Pro features: Unlimited board sync, automatic portion scaling for 2, 4, or 6 people, full nutritional macro totals, and shared family baskets.
  • ✔ Milestone: 1,200 paying subscribers = £4,788/month pure MRR.
PHASE 4 // MONTHS 6–12 ENTERPRISE SCALE

FMCG Retail Media & Sponsored Ingredients

This is how food apps like Jow reach $10M+ valuations. Major brands pay to have their branded product pre-selected as the default ingredient.

  • ✔ Partner with FMCG brands (Knorr, Hellmann's, Lurpak, Philadelphia, Heinz).
  • ✔ When a recipe calls for "mayo", Hellmann's pays £0.15 to be the default selected SKU in the Tesco/Sainsbury's basket.
  • ✔ Milestone: Total Venture Run Rate exceeds £15,000 to £20,000 / month.
