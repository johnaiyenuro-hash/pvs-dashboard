Supermarket Measures

Recipes use fuzzy words like "a handful of coriander", "2 dollops of yogurt", or "3 medium onions". The engine automatically translates these into real grocery units, grams, and standard UK supermarket pack sizes.

Money Trap #1 // AI Bill Spike

The "600-Pin Video" Cost Bomb

Video & image AI costs real pennies every time it runs. If a free user pastes a board of 600 cooking videos and never pays you, your Gemini API bill can explode overnight.

Fix: Cache every scanned URL in your database (scan once, reuse for everyone) + set fair-use caps (e.g. 20 scans/mo free, 150/mo Pro).

Money Trap #2 // Churn ("Leaky Bucket")

People Cancel Once Pins Are Saved

In consumer food apps, ~10–15% of subscribers cancel each month. At 10% churn, keeping 1,500 subscribers means finding 150 brand-new payers every single month just to stay flat.

Fix: Give a weekly reason to stay: live supermarket price-drop comparison + "What's left in my fridge" meal suggestions.

Money Trap #3 // Affiliate Myth

Bounties Only Pay Once Per Shopper

Supermarkets make tiny profits (~3%), so they only pay £5–£10 bounties for a first-time online shopper—not every week when the same person re-orders.

Fix: Treat subscriptions (Consumer + Creator) as your steady paycheck, and use "Switch & Save" + meal kits for bonus affiliate cash.

5. Commercial Risk Radar & Competitive Edge

Real-world UK market blockers to fix early so growth doesn't get derailed

5 Critical Checks
Risk / Blind Spot Level Why It Matters (Plain English) Simple Action Plan
1. Pinterest Trademark & Handle Ban HIGH Pinterest's legal rules forbid using "Pin", "Pinterest", or their brand red in third-party app names, URLs, or social handles. They can shut down your account right after you go viral. Pick a clean ownable name (e.g. Save2Plate or Board2Basket) before launching socials. Say "Works with Pinterest" instead.
2. Mobile-to-Desktop Checkout Drop-Off HIGH 85%+ of people clicking from Pinterest/TikTok/IG are on phones. Chrome extensions don't work on mobile phones or inside supermarket apps, causing huge drop-offs. Make the mobile web checklist + 1-tap supermarket search/deep-link super smooth first; treat the Chrome extension as a desktop bonus.
3. Crowded UK Competitors MED-HIGH Remy, Cherrypick, Lollipop, ReciMe & Samsung Food (free) already turn recipes into UK Tesco/Sainsbury's/Asda baskets. Win on 3 gaps they miss: (1) Bulk rescue of entire Pinterest boards, (2) Cheapest UK basket comparison, (3) Creator shoppable links.
4. Closed Whisk API & Extension Affiliate Rules MED-HIGH Whisk/Samsung Food stopped accepting new developer API apps, and Awin/CJ often reject browser extensions for cookie-tracking rules. Keep affiliate links on your website (not inside the extension) and rely on your own clean ingredient-to-search mapper.
5. UK Law: Allergens, Ads & Company Shield MEDIUM AI can misread allergens or calories; hiding paid brand swaps breaks UK ASA ad rules; taking payments in your personal name risks personal liability. Add clear allergen/macro disclaimers, badge paid brands as "Sponsored", and register a UK Ltd company before scaling paid plans.
