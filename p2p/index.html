2. Vision Ingestion Pipeline (How It Works)

From messy recipe image or link into ready-to-buy UK supermarket baskets in 1.2 seconds

5-Stage Automated Engine
01

Scan Any Recipe

Image to Text

Paste a Pinterest board URL, pin link, recipe screenshot, or photo of a recipe card. The vision model instantly reads every ingredient and amount without any manual typing.

02

Standardize Amounts

Supermarket Measures

Recipes use fuzzy words like "a handful of coriander", "2 dollops of yogurt", or "3 medium onions". The engine automatically translates these into real grocery units, grams, and standard UK supermarket pack sizes.

03

Smart Deduplication

Combine Overlap

When you pick 3 or 4 recipes for the week, they often share ingredients like garlic, olive oil, and onions. The engine combines them into a single consolidated grocery list so you never buy duplicates or waste money.

04

Cupboard Filter

Pantry Staples

Nobody wants to buy salt, black pepper, cooking oil, or flour every time they cook. The system automatically identifies common household pantry staples and keeps them off your bill unless you explicitly ask for them.

05

1-Click Cart Injection

Tesco • Sainsbury's • Asda

Every item is matched directly to in-stock supermarket products. With one click, your weekly shopping basket is filled and ready for checkout on your chosen supermarket's site, earning affiliate bounties per basket.

End-to-End Processing Speed: 1.2 Seconds per complex visual recipe
Supported Formats: Pinterest Boards Recipe Pins Food Blogs Recipe Cards
